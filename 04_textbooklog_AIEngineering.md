# Textbook Log: AI Engineering (cont.)

## The role of AI vs. humans in the application

Four dichotomies for scoping how much responsibility AI takes on:

- **Critical or complementary**: is AI essential to the product's core function, or an enhancement on top of a product that works without it?
- **Reactive or proactive**: does AI only respond to user input, or does it initiate actions/suggestions on its own?
- **Dynamic or static**: does the AI's behavior/output keep updating with new data and context, or is it fixed after deployment?
- **Human in the loop**: does a human review or approve AI outputs before they take effect, or does the AI act autonomously?

## AI product defensibility

The low barrier to entry cuts both ways — it's easy for you to build, but just as easy for competitors to copy.

There are generally three types of competitive advantage: **technology**, **data**, **distribution**.

## User feedback

User feedback matters because it's the data you need to improve the product.

Key metrics:

- **TTFT** (Time To First Token)
- **TPOT** (Time Per Output Token)
- **Total latency** — how acceptable it is for the use case
- **Quality metrics**
- **Cost metrics**

## Evaluation

Evaluation results can change your goals — don't treat the original goal as fixed once you start measuring.

The journey from 0 to 60% is easy; 60% to 100% becomes exceedingly challenging.
